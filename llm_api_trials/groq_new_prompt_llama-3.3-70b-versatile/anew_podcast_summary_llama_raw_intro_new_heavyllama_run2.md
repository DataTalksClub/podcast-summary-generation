**Title**
Digital Warehousing and FinOps Evolution Strategies

**Episode Summary**
The episode explores the transition from supply chain management to digital warehousing and FinOps, discussing key challenges and opportunities in this shift. The conversation highlights the importance of skill adaptation and the role of tools like Excel, Docker, and Terraform in data engineering. Key takeaways include the need for continuous learning and the importance of understanding business requirements and technical aspects to achieve cost-effective cloud usage.

**Guest Introduction**
Eddy Zulkifly is a staff data engineer with over 10 years of experience in data roles, specializing in data analytics and FinOps. His expertise in supply chain analytics and data engineering makes him a relevant guest for discussing digital warehousing and FinOps.

**Main Themes**
- Transitioning to digital warehousing requires adapting existing skills to new technologies.
    * Transitioning from supply chain to data engineering involves new challenges and opportunities.
    * Excel remains a fundamental tool in data analysis and business operations.
    * Docker and Terraform are essential for infrastructure management and deployment.
    * Understanding the command line is key to mastering data engineering tools.
- Data engineering involves a wide range of tools and technologies to optimize cloud costs.
    * Data warehousing is a critical aspect of data processing and FinOps.
    * Setting up ingestion systems is vital for data processing and cost optimization.
    * Performing transformations and creating visualizations are key steps in data processing.
    * Data quality is essential for reliable and actionable insights.
- FinOps is crucial for optimizing cloud spend and platform efficiency to achieve cost-effective cloud usage.
    * Implementing FinOps for cost optimization is crucial for efficient data processing.
    * FinOps helps streamline cost reporting across cloud platforms to enable consistent reporting.
    * Data engineers play a key role in implementing FinOps standards and best practices.
    * Cost optimization efforts are crucial for businesses to make informed decisions and reduce expenses.

**Important Software Tools**
- Excel: Universal tool for business analysis and data processing.
- Docker: Critical for building infrastructure and deploying applications.
- Terraform: Essential for managing environments and optimizing cloud costs.
