**Title**
Digital Warehousing and FinOps Evolution Strategies

**Episode Summary**
This episode explores the transition from supply chain management to digital warehousing and FinOps, highlighting key challenges and opportunities. The discussion focuses on the importance of data engineering in optimizing cloud spend and ensuring efficient platform operations. The episode also delves into the skills required for a successful transition, including proficiency in tools like Docker and Terraform, and understanding the fundamentals of data management and cost optimization.

**Guest Introduction**
Eddy Zulkifly is a staff data engineer at Kinaxis, with over 10 years of experience in the data field, specializing in e-commerce and supply chain analytics. Alexey is also a staff data engineer with expertise in data analytics and cloud computing, having worked at Home Depot and other companies, bringing valuable insights into the world of digital warehousing and FinOps.

**Main Themes**
* Digital warehousing is a process of organizing and managing data, similar to physical warehousing.
    + The role of data engineering in digital warehousing is crucial for optimizing cloud spend.
    + Understanding business requirements is crucial in digital warehousing and FinOps, enabling effective data management and cost optimization.
    + FinOps plays a critical role in optimizing cloud costs, enabling businesses to make the most of their cloud investments.
    + Data warehouses are designed to store and manage large amounts of data, making it easily accessible.
* FinOps and Cost Optimization is crucial for cloud infrastructure.
    + FinOps streamlines cost optimization efforts in cloud infrastructure.
    + Standardizing cost reporting is essential for consistent analysis.
    + Data engineers play a key role in implementing FinOps practices.
    + Organizations can benefit from merging data seamlessly for reporting.
* Data Engineering and FinOps have similar process focuses.
    + Data engineering and FinOps share similar process-focused methodologies.
    + DataOps practices are also mirrored in FinOps processes.
    + Using CI/CD pipelines validates datasets and checks new data impacts.
    + Employing similar methodologies is crucial for efficient data management.
