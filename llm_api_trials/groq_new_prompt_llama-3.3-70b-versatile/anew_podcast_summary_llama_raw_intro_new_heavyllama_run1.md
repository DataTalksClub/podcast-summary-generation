**Title**
Digital Warehousing and FinOps Strategies

**Episode Summary**
This episode explores the intersection of supply chain management, digital warehousing, and FinOps, highlighting key challenges and opportunities in these fields. The conversation delves into the importance of data analysis and engineering in optimizing business processes, with a focus on the transition from traditional supply chain management to digital warehousing. Key takeaways include the value of foundational knowledge in data analysis and the need for a mindset shift when adapting to new technologies like Docker and Terraform.

**Guest Introduction**
Eddy Zulkifly is a staff data engineer at Kinaxis with over 10 years of experience in the data field, bringing expertise in data engineering, e-commerce, and supply chain analytics. Eddy's experience makes him a relevant guest for discussions on digital warehousing and FinOps, offering valuable insights into the intersection of technology and business operations.

**Main Themes**
* FinOps and Digital Warehousing Strategies Are Evolving.
  * Digital warehousing is changing the supply chain management landscape.
  * Data analysis is crucial for optimizing digital warehousing processes.
  * FinOps plays a key role in ensuring efficient cloud spend and platform operations.
  * Data engineering skills are necessary for building robust data platforms.
* Key Skills for Data Engineering and Analysis.
  * Foundational knowledge in data analysis is vital for transition to data engineering.
  * Adapting to new technologies requires a mindset shift and practice.
  * Data engineering involves building data sets and dashboards for business optimization.
  * Excel skills are still valuable in data analysis and engineering.
* Transitioning to Data Engineering.
  * A data analyst background can make the transition to data engineering easier.
  * Understanding data flows and business needs is essential for data engineering.
  * Shifting from front-end roles to back-end processes requires learning new skills.

**Important Software Tools**
* Excel: Universal language for business data analysis.
* Docker: Tool for building and managing containerized applications.
* Terraform: Infrastructure as code tool for managing environments.
* Tableau: Data visualization platform for creating interactive dashboards.
* Alteryx: Low-code data analysis platform for quick analysis.
