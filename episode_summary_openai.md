**Title**  
Transforming Supply Chain Operations: The Role of FinOps and Data Engineering

**Episode Summary**  
This episode focuses on the shift from traditional supply chain management to digital operations using FinOps principles. Highlights include the importance of data analytics for operational optimization and the need for professionals to adapt to evolving technology and strategies.

**Guest Introduction**  
Eddy Zulkifly is a Staff Data Engineer at Kinaxis, specializing in data platform development. With extensive experience in supply chain analytics, Eddy provides vital insights into the intersection of data engineering and FinOps principles in modern organizational frameworks.

**Main Themes**  

**The Transformation of Supply Chain Data Roles**  
- Data professionals often begin their careers in analytics or supply chain management roles.  
- Transitioning to data engineering involves overcoming specific challenges and embracing new learning opportunities.  
- Gaining a comprehensive understanding of data flow within systems is essential for all data roles.  
- Career advancement often necessitates a shift in focus from frontend analytics to backend processing.

**Utilizing Technology for Digital Advancement**  
- Traditional tools like Excel remain vital for operational analysis in data-driven environments.  
- Proficiency in contemporary technologies, such as Docker and Terraform, is increasingly essential for data roles.  
- Automating processes with modern tools greatly enhances efficiency and streamlines operational workflows.  
- An adaptable mindset is crucial for keeping pace with rapid technological changes.

**FinOps-Navigating Financial Accountability in Cloud Operations**  
- FinOps promotes financial accountability in cloud environments, aligning technology with business objectives.  
- Cultivating a culture of optimization allows organizations to maximize their cloud investments effectively.  
- Data engineers play a significant role in designing analytics processes that manage cloud spending.  
- Accurate data sets reflecting financial performance inform crucial decision-making for business strategies.

**Important Software Tools**  
- **Excel**: A fundamental tool for data analysis that remains crucial for reporting tasks.  
- **Alteryx**: A low-code analytics platform that enhances rapid data processing capabilities.  
- **Docker**: Enables application development and deployment in isolated environments for better management.  
- **Terraform**: Automates infrastructure management, making it easier to set up environments efficiently.
