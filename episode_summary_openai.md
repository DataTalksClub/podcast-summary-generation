**Title**  
Transforming Supply Chain Management: Insights on Digital Solutions and FinOps

**Episode Summary**  
This episode explores the critical shift from traditional supply chain management to digital solutions and FinOps strategies. Key takeaways include the significance of foundational analytics skills for data engineering roles and effective techniques for reducing cloud expenses.

**Guest Introduction**  
Eddy Zulkifly is a Staff Data Engineer at Kinaxis, focused on advancing digital warehousing and financial operations through data solutions. His extensive background in supply chain analytics equips him with valuable perspectives on digital transformation in logistics and finance.

**Main Themes**  

**Navigating the Shift from Analytics to Data Engineering Requires Key Skills**  
- A strong foundation in data analytics is essential for grasping data flow in organizations.  
- Skills in creating dashboards provide a helpful transition to developing data pipelines efficiently.  
- Data analysts can smoothly transition into data engineering by understanding backend data processing.  
- Robust data management is crucial for informed decision-making and optimizing operational processes.

**Embracing Technical Tools to Overcome Challenges**  
- Command-line tools such as Docker and Terraform can initially intimidate data analysts.  
- Mastering one technology can simplify the learning curve for additional tools and languages.  
- A focus on fundamental principles supports smoother transitions to complex data systems.  
- Organizations benefit from training programs that close skill gaps in emerging tech frameworks.

**FinOps: Optimizing Resource Management in Digital Environments**  
- Optimization of physical warehouse space parallels the management of digital infrastructure.  
- Data analytics plays a significant role in predicting cloud resource spending accurately.  
- Tools like Excel remain vital for decision-making despite the rise of sophisticated software.  
- Automation of processes enhances efficiency and streamlines workflows in operational functions.

**Important Software Tools**  
- **Alteryx**: A low-code platform that simplifies data preparation and analysis for business needs.  
- **Tableau**: An interactive data visualization tool that helps in creating insightful dashboards.  
- **Docker**: A containerization platform that facilitates application deployment and management.  
- **Terraform**: An infrastructure as code tool that automates cloud resource management and creation.
