**Title**
Digital Warehousing and FinOps Evolution Insights

**Episode Summary**
The episode discusses the evolution of supply chain management, digital warehousing, and FinOps, highlighting key challenges and opportunities. Two main highlights include transitioning from traditional supply chain management to digital warehousing and developing skills in tools like Docker and Terraform. The conversation also touches on the role of data analysis in optimizing business processes.

**Guest Introduction**
Eddy Zulkifly is a staff data engineer with expertise in e-commerce and supply chain analytics. Eddy's experience makes him a relevant guest for this episode's topic. Eddy is also a mentor and teaching assistant.

**Main Themes**
1. Career Development in Data Engineering is crucial for success.
    * Data engineers need to develop skills in tools like Docker and Terraform.
    * Transitioning from data analysis to data engineering requires a mindset shift.
    * Understanding the fundamentals of one tool makes it easier to learn others.
    * Data engineers must be comfortable with the command line and scripting.
2. Digital Warehousing and Supply Chain Optimization are interconnected processes.
    * Digital warehousing involves optimizing cloud spend and ensuring platform efficiency.
    * Supply chain optimization requires analyzing data to forecast demand and manage inventory.
    * Technology like Excel macros and Alteryx can be used for optimization.
    * Data analysis plays a critical role in optimizing business processes.
3. Data Analysis and Engineering are essential for Business Success and growth.
    * Data analysis is vital for understanding business needs and optimizing processes.
    * Data engineering involves building data pipelines and automating workflows.
    * Tools like Tableau and Power BI are used for data analysis and visualization.
    * Understanding data flows and business needs is crucial for data engineers.

**Important Software Tools**
* Excel: analysis and optimization tool.
* Docker: infrastructure building tool.
* Terraform: infrastructure management tool.
* Tableau: data analysis and visualization tool.
* Alteryx: data analysis and optimization tool.
