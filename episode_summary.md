**Title**
Digital Warehousing and FinOps Strategies Explored

**Episode Summary**
This episode explores the transition from supply chain management to digital warehousing and FinOps, highlighting key challenges and opportunities. The main takeaways include the importance of foundational knowledge in data analysis and adapting to new tools and technologies. The conversation also delves into the role of data engineering in optimizing cloud spend and platform efficiency. Key insights are provided on the similarities between physical and digital warehousing, emphasizing the need for understanding business requirements and utilizing tools like metric trees and data specs.

**Guest Introduction**
Eddy Zulkifly is a staff data engineer with over 10 years of experience in the data field, specializing in e-commerce and supply chain analytics. Eddy Zulkifly brings expertise in data engineering and cloud cost optimization to the conversation, providing valuable insights into digital warehousing and FinOps.

**Main Themes**
* Building a Career in Data Engineering is crucial for success.
    * Foundational knowledge in data analysis is essential for a career in data engineering.
    * Adapting to new tools and technologies is vital for professional growth.
    * Data engineering requires a strong understanding of backend processes.
    * Automation and optimization are key aspects of data engineering.
* Supply Chain Management and Digital Warehousing share common goals.
    * Optimizing space in warehouses is a crucial aspect of supply chain management.
    * Digital warehousing applies similar principles to optimize cloud spend.
    * Data analysis and interpretation are critical skills in both fields.
    * Technology plays a significant role in both areas.
* Implementing FinOps in cloud infrastructure is crucial for cost optimization.
    * FinOps involves streamlining cost reporting and leveraging tools.
    * Data engineers play a key role in building robust data platforms.
    * Cost optimization efforts require a deep understanding of cloud infrastructure.
    * Implementing FinOps standards can lead to significant cost savings.
