**Title**
Digital Warehousing and FinOps Insights

**Episode Summary**
The episode explores digital warehousing and FinOps, highlighting key challenges and opportunities. Two main takeaways include the importance of foundational knowledge in data analysis and the need for a mindset shift when working with new tools. The conversation delves into the guest's career journey, showcasing the application of data skills in various roles. Key themes include digital warehousing, data analysis, and transitioning to data engineering. The episode also discusses the importance of cost optimization and efficient data management.

**Guest Introduction**
Eddy Zulkifly is a staff data engineer at Kinaxis, specializing in e-commerce and supply chain analytics, with over 10 years of experience in the data field. Alexey is a staff data engineer with expertise in data analytics and cloud computing, bringing valuable insights to the episode.

* Digital warehousing is transforming supply chain management and optimizing cloud spend.

* Data analysis is foundational for career growth and applicable across industries.
    * Data skills are valuable in both technical and non-technical roles.
    * Career growth in data analysis requires continuous learning and professional development.
    * Data analysis involves working with various tools and technologies.

* Transitioning to data engineering requires a mindset shift and continuous learning.
    * Data engineering involves working with new tools and technologies.
